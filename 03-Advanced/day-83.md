## Theme — Refine through Domain Architecture

**Quote of the day:** “Elegance is not merely a technique; it is a way to turn attention into responsible architectural judgment.”

**Mindset:** Refine a solution until usefulness and restraint reinforce each other. In Domain Architecture, practice this through domain language, policies and bounded contexts.

## Skill of the day — Elegance in context

**Scenario:** Teams disagree about the meaning of an order. The immediate response may miss the underlying system. Examine shared definitions, domain ownership and invariants; separate what is observed from what is inferred, and identify who is affected.

**Architect's question:** Does the design solve the problem with minimal avoidable friction?

## Practical framework — S.I.G.N.

1. **S — Signal:** Write the observable fact without embellishment.
2. **I — Interpret:** Connect the signal to domain language, policies and bounded contexts.
3. **G — Ground:** Seek evidence from shared definitions, domain ownership and invariants and test an alternative explanation.
4. **N — Next move:** Choose one proportionate experiment, decision or conversation.

## Five-minute micro-experiment

Compare two designs for clarity, resilience and maintainability.

- **1 minute:** Record the evidence you actually have.
- **2 minutes:** Apply S.I.G.N. to the domain scenario.
- **1 minute:** Identify a stakeholder and a trade-off.
- **1 minute:** Write the smallest useful next action.
