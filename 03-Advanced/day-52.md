## Theme — Illuminate through Application Architecture

**Quote of the day:** “Clarity is not merely a technique; it is a way to turn attention into responsible architectural judgment.”

**Mindset:** Illuminate complex trade-offs so others can act wisely. In Application Architecture, practice this through software boundaries, behaviors and change.

## Skill of the day — Clarity in context

**Scenario:** A small release breaks neighboring services. The immediate response may miss the underlying system. Examine coupling, contracts and regression evidence; separate what is observed from what is inferred, and identify who is affected.

**Architect's question:** What must another person understand to act well?

## Practical framework — S.I.G.N.

1. **S — Signal:** Write the observable fact without embellishment.
2. **I — Interpret:** Connect the signal to software boundaries, behaviors and change.
3. **G — Ground:** Seek evidence from coupling, contracts and regression evidence and test an alternative explanation.
4. **N — Next move:** Choose one proportionate experiment, decision or conversation.

## Five-minute micro-experiment

Explain the issue in two sentences; note what remains uncertain.

- **1 minute:** Record the evidence you actually have.
- **2 minutes:** Apply S.I.G.N. to the application scenario.
- **1 minute:** Identify a stakeholder and a trade-off.
- **1 minute:** Write the smallest useful next action.
