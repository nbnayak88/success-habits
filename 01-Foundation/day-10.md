## Theme — See through Integration Architecture

**Quote of the day:** “Awareness is not merely a technique; it is a way to turn attention into responsible architectural judgment.”

**Mindset:** Notice the assumptions shaping your first reaction. In Integration Architecture, practice this through end-to-end flow, contracts and interoperability.

## Skill of the day — Awareness in context

**Scenario:** Messages succeed but business documents are missing. The immediate response may miss the underlying system. Examine correlation IDs, retries and business reconciliation; separate what is observed from what is inferred, and identify who is affected.

**Architect's question:** What am I overlooking because it feels familiar?

## Practical framework — S.I.G.N.

1. **S — Signal:** Write the observable fact without embellishment.
2. **I — Interpret:** Connect the signal to end-to-end flow, contracts and interoperability.
3. **G — Ground:** Seek evidence from correlation IDs, retries and business reconciliation and test an alternative explanation.
4. **N — Next move:** Choose one proportionate experiment, decision or conversation.

## Five-minute micro-experiment

Name one hidden assumption and compare it with observable evidence.

- **1 minute:** Record the evidence you actually have.
- **2 minutes:** Apply S.I.G.N. to the integration scenario.
- **1 minute:** Identify a stakeholder and a trade-off.
- **1 minute:** Write the smallest useful next action.

## Intention

## Visualization

## Journal reflection
