## Theme — Own through Data Architecture

**Quote of the day:** “Responsibility is not merely a technique; it is a way to turn attention into responsible architectural judgment.”

**Mindset:** Own consequences across the full lifecycle. In Data Architecture, practice this through semantics, lineage and information quality.

## Skill of the day — Responsibility in context

**Scenario:** Two dashboards report different revenue. The immediate response may miss the underlying system. Examine metric definitions, lineage and source reconciliation; separate what is observed from what is inferred, and identify who is affected.

**Architect's question:** What accountability remains after delivery?

## Practical framework — S.I.G.N.

1. **S — Signal:** Write the observable fact without embellishment.
2. **I — Interpret:** Connect the signal to semantics, lineage and information quality.
3. **G — Ground:** Seek evidence from metric definitions, lineage and source reconciliation and test an alternative explanation.
4. **N — Next move:** Choose one proportionate experiment, decision or conversation.

## Five-minute micro-experiment

Name an owner, an outcome metric and an escalation route.

- **1 minute:** Record the evidence you actually have.
- **2 minutes:** Apply S.I.G.N. to the data scenario.
- **1 minute:** Identify a stakeholder and a trade-off.
- **1 minute:** Write the smallest useful next action.
