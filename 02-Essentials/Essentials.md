# Essentials — 84-Day Habit Practice

**Question:** How should I work?  
**Mission:** Architecting Experiences for a Better World.

Seven habits × twelve architecture streams = **84 daily cards**. Each card includes a quote, architectural scenario, skill, S.I.G.N. framework, five-minute practice, intention, affirmation, visualization, gratitude, reflection and next-day navigation.

**Habits:** Observation · Interpretation · Structuring · Prioritization · Decision-Making · Communication · Collaboration
