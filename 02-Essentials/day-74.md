## Theme — Connect through Business Architecture

**Quote of the day:** “Collaboration is not merely a technique; it is a way to turn attention into responsible architectural judgment.”

**Mindset:** Connect perspectives to produce a stronger shared design. In Business Architecture, practice this through capabilities, value streams and accountability.

## Skill of the day — Collaboration in context

**Scenario:** Customer onboarding takes too long. The immediate response may miss the underlying system. Examine handoffs, business rules and ownership boundaries; separate what is observed from what is inferred, and identify who is affected.

**Architect's question:** Whose knowledge is missing from this decision?

## Practical framework — S.I.G.N.

1. **S — Signal:** Write the observable fact without embellishment.
2. **I — Interpret:** Connect the signal to capabilities, value streams and accountability.
3. **G — Ground:** Seek evidence from handoffs, business rules and ownership boundaries and test an alternative explanation.
4. **N — Next move:** Choose one proportionate experiment, decision or conversation.

## Five-minute micro-experiment

Invite one stakeholder to challenge an assumption and capture the change.

- **1 minute:** Record the evidence you actually have.
- **2 minutes:** Apply S.I.G.N. to the business scenario.
- **1 minute:** Identify a stakeholder and a trade-off.
- **1 minute:** Write the smallest useful next action.

## Intention

“Today I will practice collaboration deliberately, with curiosity and care.”

## Affirmation

“I can grow my business judgment through small, evidence-based actions.”

## Visualization

Imagine explaining your reasoning to a cross-functional team. Instead of asserting an answer, you show the signal, the possible meaning, the evidence still needed and a safe next step.

## Gratitude

“I am grateful for the chance to learn from real systems and the people who use them.”

## Journal reflection

**Whose knowledge is missing from this decision?** What did you learn, and what would you change tomorrow?

## Architect upgrade

> **The strongest Business Architect combines collaboration with evidence, empathy and action.**
