## Theme — Organize through Infrastructure Architecture

**Quote of the day:** “Structuring is not merely a technique; it is a way to turn attention into responsible architectural judgment.”

**Mindset:** Arrange complexity into relationships people can reason about. In Infrastructure Architecture, practice this through resilience, capacity and service continuity.

## Skill of the day — Structuring in context

**Scenario:** Latency spikes during peak demand. The immediate response may miss the underlying system. Examine capacity signals, failure domains and recovery objectives; separate what is observed from what is inferred, and identify who is affected.

**Architect's question:** Which boundaries, flows and dependencies organize this problem?

## Practical framework — S.I.G.N.

1. **S — Signal:** Write the observable fact without embellishment.
2. **I — Interpret:** Connect the signal to resilience, capacity and service continuity.
3. **G — Ground:** Seek evidence from capacity signals, failure domains and recovery objectives and test an alternative explanation.
4. **N — Next move:** Choose one proportionate experiment, decision or conversation.

## Five-minute micro-experiment

Sketch actors, components and dependencies on one page.

- **1 minute:** Record the evidence you actually have.
- **2 minutes:** Apply S.I.G.N. to the infrastructure scenario.
- **1 minute:** Identify a stakeholder and a trade-off.
- **1 minute:** Write the smallest useful next action.
