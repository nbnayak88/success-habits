# Career Architecture Case: Senior HRBP in Business Transformation

> **Illustrative case profile:** A professional with approximately 20 years of experience at Infosys, working as an HR Business Partner (HRBP) supporting Business Transformation Management (BTM), with a current CTC of approximately **₹36 lakh per annum (LPA)**.

**Purpose:** Show how a Career Success Architect diagnoses career positioning and designs a practical transition. This is a generalized learning case; no individual is identified or assessed.

## One career promise

**Move from HR support and execution toward measurable ownership of people and organizational transformation.**

At this stage, the goal should be to increase scope, influence, and proof of outcomes—not simply obtain another designation.

## Potential career pathways

| Pathway | Why it may fit | Illustrative India CTC negotiation range* |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| HR Transformation Director | Bridges HRBP and cross-enterprise transformation | ₹45–65 LPA |
| Strategic HRBP / HR Director | Builds on large-scale business partnering | ₹45–60 LPA |
| People & Organization Transformation Leader | Focus on operating-model and workforce change | ₹50–75 LPA |
| Change Management / Transformation Director | Leads adoption and transformation outcomes | ₹45–70 LPA |
| CHRO / Head of HR, mid-sized enterprise | Possible if full-spectrum HR leadership is proven | ₹60 LPA–₹1 crore+ |

*These are **illustrative, unverified negotiation hypotheses**, **not current market salary benchmarks**, quotes, or guaranteed offers. Verify live market data by city, industry, employer, scope, fixed/variable split, and the individual's delivery record before using them.*

## Recommended initial direction

**HR Transformation Director / People Transformation Leader**

Potential capabilities to emphasize:

- Enterprise organization design and operating-model redesign
- Strategic workforce and succession planning
- AI-enabled HR operating models and responsible automation
- Change leadership and adoption measurement
- Leadership development and talent strategy
- Business-case development, value realization, and ROI

**Important diagnostic fork:** Does the professional support a BTM business unit as HRBP, or directly lead HR/business transformation initiatives? The answer materially changes readiness for a transformation-director role.

## 90-day experiment

| Period | Focus | Tangible evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | **Reposition**: inventory achievements and define target leadership scope | Executive narrative; 3–5 quantified STAR stories |
| Days 31–60 | **Prove**: build a leadership portfolio from real experiences | Case studies on workforce strategy, organization design, change and measurable impact |
| Days 61–90 | **Test the market**: conduct targeted conversations and role interviews | Role-fit feedback; tailored CV; executive case presentations |

### Compensation hypothesis

Moving from **₹36 LPA** to **₹50–60 LPA** implies approximately **39–67%** higher nominal CTC. Such a jump is aspirational and more plausible with expanded leadership ownership than a routine lateral HRBP move. Evaluate the **fixed salary, variable pay, equity, location, role scope, and long-term progression**, not CTC alone.

## Career Success Architect diagnostic questions

1. What transformation outcomes has this person personally owned?
2. How many employees, business units, geographies, and leaders have they supported?
3. Which achievements can be evidenced with metrics?
4. Is their experience primarily business partnering, HR operations, program change, or strategic transformation?
5. Which leadership responsibilities do they want next—and why?

## Five-minute reflection

> What business problem can I now credibly own end-to-end, beyond the responsibilities in my present job description?

**Architectural principle:** Career success is not measured only by tenure or certifications. It is strengthened through clarity, demonstrated capability, and sustained impact.
